## Source priority

Use sources in this order:

1. New user-provided experience and evidence.
2. Unused historical material or content-bank entries.
3. Questions repeatedly faced by the target audience.
4. Verified public cases, products, and current developments.
5. Evergreen methods and proposed viewpoints aligned with the creator profile.

Do not skip directly to trends when personal material can support a stronger piece.

## Extract content atoms

For source-rich or source-light work, extract:

- event or observation;
- audience problem;
- tension, surprise, or failed expectation;
- decision and alternatives;
- process or tool;
- evidence: screenshot, quote, number, artifact, or observable outcome;
- result and remaining uncertainty;
- lesson or changed belief;
- safe-to-share boundary;
- possible follow-up questions.

Do not require every atom. Missing evidence must reduce certainty, not invite invention.

## Expand one strong source

Create derivatives only when each has a different reader promise. Typical angles:

1. Story: what happened and why it mattered.
2. Problem: why the underlying user pain exists.
3. Decision: how alternatives were evaluated.
4. Workflow: the repeatable steps or AI setup.
5. Failure: what did not work and what changed.
6. Side-project lens: time, energy, scope, or opportunity cost.
7. Long-form synthesis: complete reasoning for WeChat.

Do not publish all derivatives at once. Place unused angles in the content bank.

## Generate content without recent progress

Choose one of these honest formats:

- **Audience question:** answer a concrete obstacle faced by the target audience.
- **Method:** explain a repeatable framework the user has used or explicitly approves.
- **Public case analysis:** analyze a product, creator, workflow, or market change with sources.
- **Proposed viewpoint:** offer 3–5 defensible positions for user confirmation before writing in first person.
- **Evergreen guide:** solve a searchable problem within the account positioning.
- **Historical retrospective:** reuse an older real event while stating its timing honestly.
- **Curated comparison:** compare tools or approaches with transparent criteria and current verification.

Never convert a public case into “我的经历.” Never manufacture a build log because the account has been quiet.

The absence of progress can occasionally support an honest piece about energy, recovery, scope, or sustainable creative systems. Use it only when the angle is new and actionable. Check recent topics first; if a similar停滞、断更、重启 piece already exists, choose an audience question, method, public case, or evergreen guide instead.

## Candidate scoring

Score each candidate out of 100:

- Audience pain or desire: 25
- Creator fit and personal distinctiveness: 25
- Evidence or demonstrability: 20
- Clear practical payoff: 15
- Series and reuse potential: 15

Interpretation:

- 80–100: priority candidate;
- 65–79: strengthen the angle or evidence;
- below 65: archive or discard.

Break ties by choosing the candidate with stronger truthful evidence, not the more sensational title.

## Sustainable content mix

Use a rolling-month mix that fits the creator profile. A reasonable starting point is:

- 35% real stories and decisions;
- 30% practical workflows;
- 20% audience questions and evergreen guides;
- 15% opinions or public case analyses.

If recent progress is limited, replace current stories with historical retrospectives and public case analyses; do not imitate a daily build diary.

## Content-bank entry

Represent each reusable item with:

```markdown
### [Short source name]
- Captured: YYYY-MM-DD
- Source type: new experience | historical | audience question | public research | viewpoint proposal
- Verified facts:
- Core tension:
- Audience value:
- Evidence/assets:
- Safe-to-share limits:
- Candidate angles:
- Used outputs:
- Remaining derivatives:
- Status: inbox | ready | drafted | published | exhausted
```

When persistent storage is available, update the existing content bank rather than creating duplicate files. Otherwise return this block so the user can save it.
