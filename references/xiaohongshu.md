# Xiaohongshu guide

## Choose the post promise

Make each post deliver one primary value:

- recognition: “this is exactly my problem”;
- utility: “I can use this method”;
- discovery: “I did not know this was possible”;
- judgment: “this gives me a better way to decide”;
- story: “I want to know what happened next.”

Do not combine several weak promises.

## Build the carousel

Default to 6–9 cards when the user does not specify a count.

1. **Cover:** one clear tension or outcome; concise enough to read at thumbnail size.
2. **Context:** who faced the problem and why it matters.
3. **Conflict:** failed expectation, obstacle, or overlooked cost.
4. **Process:** concrete action, comparison, or framework.
5. **Evidence:** screenshot, artifact, example, or transparent source.
6. **Insight:** what changed in the creator's understanding.
7. **Action:** what the reader can try or avoid.
8. **Continuation:** next experiment or a specific discussion question.

Use fewer cards when the idea is simple. Give every card a short heading and focused body copy. Avoid turning cards into dense article pages.

## Write titles

Generate 5–10 meaningfully different titles when title options are requested. Use credible tension:

- unexpected obstacle;
- before/after judgment;
- cost of a common assumption;
- specific audience + concrete problem;
- a decision the reader also faces.

Do not use unsupported superlatives, guaranteed outcomes, fake urgency, or title-content mismatch. Prefer a specific honest claim over “绝了”“封神”“必看.”

## Write the caption

- Open with the real context rather than repeating the cover.
- Add details that would overcrowd the cards.
- Use short paragraphs and natural spoken Chinese.
- End with one answerable question tied to the post, not generic engagement bait.
- Add only relevant topic tags; do not stuff unrelated trending tags.

## Provide a visual brief

For each card, specify the primary visual source:

- real screenshot;
- product mockup;
- highlighted quote;
- comparison table;
- process diagram;
- simple illustration;
- text-led card.

Favor real artifacts for credibility. Redact private data. Keep a consistent grid, typography hierarchy, color system, and cover treatment across a series.

## Deliverable

Return in this order unless the user asks otherwise:

1. Recommended title and alternative titles.
2. Cover copy.
3. Card-by-card copy with visual direction.
4. Publishing caption.
5. Tags.
6. Pinned-comment or discussion prompt when useful.
7. Claims or personal details requiring confirmation.
