# WeChat Official Account guide

## Define the long-form value

Use WeChat to preserve complete reasoning, not to inflate a Xiaohongshu post. A strong article should offer at least one of:

- a full build or decision story;
- a reusable framework;
- a researched case analysis;
- a nuanced position with evidence;
- a practical guide with examples.

## Default structure

1. **Title:** concrete promise or tension, without sensational mismatch.
2. **Abstract:** explain why the reader should care.
3. **Opening scene:** begin with a real moment, question, or observation.
4. **Problem:** define the underlying conflict and stakes.
5. **Exploration:** show attempts, evidence, alternatives, and tradeoffs.
6. **Resolution:** state the current answer, including uncertainty.
7. **Reader takeaway:** convert the experience into a useful framework or next step.
8. **Closing:** connect to the creator's continuing journey and ask one meaningful question.

Adapt this structure to the material. Do not force a personal scene into public research content.

## Style

- Default to 1,500–2,500 Chinese characters when no length is specified; use only the length needed.
- Use descriptive subheadings that advance the argument.
- Mix short and medium paragraphs; avoid one-sentence fragmentation throughout.
- Prefer examples, decisions, and consequences over abstract slogans.
- Keep uncertainty visible. Distinguish facts, interpretation, and personal preference.
- Remove formulaic transitions such as repeated “首先、其次、最后” when they make the article sound generated.

## Images and formatting

Suggest image positions only when they support comprehension or credibility:

- product screenshot after the relevant decision;
- workflow diagram before a multi-step method;
- comparison table where criteria repeat;
- pull quote only for a genuinely distinctive sentence.

Provide a cover concept, cover line, abstract, and Markdown-ready body. Produce HTML only when the user requests it or a publishing workflow requires it.

When the user asks for rendered images, use `visual-production.md`. Default to a coordinated 21:9 article header and 1:1 share card. Use a single-cover engine only for a deliberately lightweight deliverable; use a social-card engine when the cover must match a wider visual series.

## Cross-platform adaptation

When adapting from Xiaohongshu:

- restore missing context and reasoning;
- add evidence and counterarguments;
- replace card repetition with a coherent narrative;
- avoid reusing the Xiaohongshu hook as every section heading.

When adapting to Xiaohongshu:

- choose one argument from the article;
- compress around a single reader promise;
- preserve the most concrete example or framework.
