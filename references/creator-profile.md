# Default creator profile template

Use this de-personalized profile as a starting point. Newer user instructions always override it. When a missing profile detail would materially change the result, ask one focused question instead of running a broad onboarding interview.

## Identity and objective

- Status: an independent or part-time creator building content, products, or professional expertise with limited time.
- Long-term objective: establish a durable personal brand rather than chase short-term traffic alone.
- Positioning: document real practice, decisions, experiments, and useful methods within the creator's chosen field.
- Credibility stance: practitioner in progress, not an all-knowing expert or get-rich-quick mentor.

Replace these defaults with the creator's real identity, objective, positioning, and credibility boundary when supplied.

## Core content pillars

Use two to four connected pillars. Suggested placeholders:

1. Practical tools and workflows.
2. Building, design, validation, or professional decisions.
3. Honest experiments, lessons, and tradeoffs.

Connect the pillars with one recognizable narrative. Do not run them as unrelated accounts.

## Platforms and formats

- Primary platforms: Xiaohongshu and WeChat Official Accounts unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Default formats: image-and-text posts, screen recordings, voiceover with materials, and long-form articles.
- Do not require face-to-camera footage unless the creator wants it.
- Use Xiaohongshu for discoverable, focused, visual ideas; use WeChat for complete reasoning and durable long-form assets.

## Visual identity

- Record the preferred visual engine, style system, accent color, typography, logo/signature, screenshot treatment, and reusable cover pattern when supplied.
- Default to structured, text-led layouts for methods and product content; use restrained editorial layouts for reflective stories.
- Keep one recognizable system across a series while adapting dimensions to each platform.
- Treat any model-proposed visual preference as a recommendation until the creator approves it.

## Voice

- Honest, calm, curious, specific, and appropriately self-reflective.
- Explain decisions and tradeoffs in plain language.
- Admit uncertainty and unfinished work.
- Prefer qualified judgments over absolute claims.
- Avoid manufactured anxiety, fake success, exaggerated earnings, empty inspiration, and preaching.

Adapt the voice to examples or explicit style constraints supplied by the user.

## Audience

Define the audience from the user's positioning. When no audience is supplied, assume:

- curious non-experts who want practical, understandable guidance;
- early practitioners who have ideas but struggle to execute;
- readers who value real workflows and honest tradeoffs over effortless-success narratives.

## Privacy and disclosure boundaries

- Keep employer, client, family, revenue, unreleased product, and identifying details private unless explicitly approved.
- Redact personal data in screenshots and artifacts.
- Never infer that a private detail is safe to publish merely because it appeared in the conversation.

## Sustainable default cadence

- One or two Xiaohongshu posts per week.
- One WeChat article every two weeks.
- Add a real progress story when a meaningful milestone occurs.

Change the cadence whenever the user gives a different target, available time, or platform priority.
